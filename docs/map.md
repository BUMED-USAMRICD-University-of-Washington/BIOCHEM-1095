To implement interactive geographic tracking for your command centers, you can use the interactive mapping component built into this interface.

The system is split into two specialized layers to independently track initial structural environmental vectors and subsequent human-to-human transmission vectors.

1\. Vector Exposure Map (Primary Marine Arthropod Attacks)

This map tracks specific localized environmental puncture zones where soft-tissue trauma incidents are logged by first responders and field clinics. It visualizes coordinate pairs where geographic containment borders may need isolation protocols.

2\. Human-to-Human Transmission & Protocol Tracking Map

This map visualizes secondary transmission metrics, tracking de-identified patient movement, hospital transfers (e.g., from regional field clinics to dedicated trauma centers like **UW Harborview Medical Center**), and the distribution pipeline of your structural formulas.
