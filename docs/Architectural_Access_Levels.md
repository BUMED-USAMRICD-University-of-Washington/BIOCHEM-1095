Part 1: Architectural Access Levels (Data Tiering)

To satisfy federal privacy laws and international compliance metrics, your operational model utilizes four tiers of data architecture:

-   **Level 1: Public Health Surveillance (Fully Anonymized).** Global and national registry tier mapping pathogen burden, strain epidemiology, and gross population recovery trends. Contains zero Patient Protected Health Information (PHI).
-   **Level 2: Research Study Network (De-Identified Token Matrix).** Used by collaborating universities, IRBs, and your open-source analytics portal. Patients are represented strictly by cryptographic tokens to evaluate broad formula efficacy across distinct cohorts.
-   **Level 3: Operational Command (Controlled Multi-State Pipeline).** Restrictive tier utilized by the **Yesler Towers Command Center** to track manufacturing lot numbers, product formulations, real-time logistics telemetry, and state-by-state physician hours.
-   **Level 4: Point-of-Care Clinical EMR (Encrypted Air-Gapped Network).** Restricted data environment containing localized patient records, clinical charts, and active physician prescribing notes. Linked to Level 2 and 3 pipelines via one-way cryptographic SHA-256 validation hashes.
