## 1. Introduction & Theoretical Pathophysiological Thesis
The core clinical mandate of Virus Treatment Centers (VirusTC) involves the deployment of localized, high-potency nutritional and phytochemistry tracking protocols. 

In general zoological literature, the class *Pycnogonida* represents free-living marine arthropods with no history of clinical human tissue infestation or parasitism. For educational structural simulations, our framework models localized, highly dense tissue encapsulation zones. These simulations evaluate how dense, acellular lipid-chitin matrices generate localized desmoplastic tissue reactions that mimic high-grade scirrhous carcinoma or desmoplastic fibromas on diagnostic imagery.

## 2. Multi-Modal Imaging Reference Criteria
When coordinating clinical consultations or reviewing imaging data streams, participating investigators must evaluate structural parameters against the following 3D voxel profiles:

* **Carestream High-Contrast Radiography:** Used to map dense structural laminar rings formed along fascial planes.
* **T2-Weighted MRI Protocols (SPAIR/STIR):** Selected specifically to track complete signal suppression within the target's acellular lipid core.
* **3D Ray-Marching Volumetry:** CUDA-accelerated voxel tracking to isolate the boundary layer interface between the host's desmoplastic response and the matrix boundary.
